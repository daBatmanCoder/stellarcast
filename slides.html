Hackathon pitch

Stellarcast

Private livestreams. Public chain. Unlinkable viewers.

Sepolia ERC-5564 stealth ENS meta-address WebRTC + TURN
The problem

Paying for a stream on Ethereum is a public confession.

Wallet = identity

One address ties every ticket, tip, and late-night show to the same person.

The ledger never forgets

Anyone can reconstruct who paid whom, when, and for what. Forever.

Platforms keep the list

Twitch already knows the audience. A public chain copies that problem, then publishes it.

The idea

Pay like cash. Watch like a ghost.

Each ticket lands on a one-time stealth address only the host can recognize. The payment proves access. It does not name the viewer.

How it works

Four steps. One payment. Then silence.

01 Discover Browse live rooms from the on-chain NFT registry.
02 Pay One MetaMask tx: ETH to a stealth address, plus an ERC-5564 announcement.
03 Unlock Host scans announcements with view tags and decrypts the ticket.
04 Watch Camera over encrypted WebRTC. TURN if the two machines cannot peer.
Architecture

The privacy boundary.

Stays on Sepolia

  • Room NFT: title, host ENS, price, live or burned
  • Stealth meta-address on ENS / ERC-6538
  • One announcement per ticket or tip
  • Host burns the NFT to end the room

Never hits the chain

  • Video frames and audio
  • WebRTC offers, ICE, TURN relay
  • Spending and viewing keys, stored locally
  • The access credential from the stealth secret

After access is granted, watching produces zero additional blockchain activity.

What's live

Shipped on Sepolia. Not a mock.

  • MetaMask, one-transaction stealth pay
  • ENS stealth key required to go live
  • ERC-5564 announcer + host scanner
  • StellaCastRooms NFT registry
  • Host dashboard, tips, paid re-entry
  • Camera over WebRTC, TURN fallback
